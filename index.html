What should you do today?

Hi, I’m Savage.

I moved to Toronto recently and figuring out cool things happening was harder than it should be. Events scattered across a dozen sites, half of them stale, none of them talking to each other.

Then I found Muharrem’s site and thought: Toronto deserves one of these. So I built it.

Everything here is free, cheap, or at least worth the fare. The calendar starts today, so if you’re asking “what should I do today?”, the first card is your answer.

Every listing is verified against the venue’s own page. Open any card and check the bottom to see which source and when. If I couldn’t confirm it, it’s not here.

Have fun!
